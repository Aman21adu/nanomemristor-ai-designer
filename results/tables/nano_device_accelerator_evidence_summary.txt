NANO-DEVICE TO ACCELERATOR EVIDENCE SUMMARY
==========================================

CURRENT SCIENTIFIC POSITION
---------------------------

The current simulator is device-electrical-behavior-aware.
It is not yet a complete material-to-device physics simulator.

Literature-derived memristor electrical characteristics constrain how neural-network weights are represented and how crossbar currents are quantized.

1. ABSOLUTE CONDUCTANCE SCALE
-----------------------------

Controlled experiment: Gmin and Gmax were multiplied by the same factor while the ON/OFF ratio was preserved.

Tested 28 cases across 4 devices.
Scale range: 0.001x to 1000x.
Passed cases: 28/28.
Maximum numerical output difference: 5.364e-07.

Interpretation: absolute conductance magnitude is effectively normalized away by the current simulator when the ON/OFF ratio is unchanged.

This is a CURRENT MODEL LIMITATION, not a claim about real hardware.

Real arrays can depend on absolute conductance through IR drop, line resistance, current magnitude, power, sensing limits and heating.

2. ON/OFF RATIO EFFECT
----------------------

Controlled experiment: only the ON/OFF conductance ratio was changed while network, crossbar size, weight precision, ADC precision, mapping mode and dataset were held fixed.

ZnO_01:
  ADC 4: ratio 2 -> 3000, accuracy 92.82% -> 95.91% (+3.09 pp).
  ADC 6: ratio 2 -> 3000, accuracy 95.91% -> 96.07% (+0.16 pp).

TaOx_01:
  ADC 4: ratio 2 -> 3000, accuracy 67.98% -> 94.47% (+26.49 pp).
  ADC 6: ratio 2 -> 3000, accuracy 95.66% -> 95.92% (+0.26 pp).

HfOx_02:
  ADC 4: ratio 2 -> 3000, accuracy 69.24% -> 94.31% (+25.07 pp).
  ADC 6: ratio 2 -> 3000, accuracy 95.66% -> 96.00% (+0.34 pp).

Interpretation: ON/OFF ratio is an active device parameter, but its accelerator-level importance depends strongly on ADC precision.

Higher ADC resolution compensates for much of the conductance-window limitation in the present model.

3. PHYSICAL CONDUCTANCE-STATE CAPABILITY
----------------------------------------

Controlled experiment: physical conductance-state count was varied while ON/OFF ratio, ADC precision, weight precision, crossbar size, network and dataset were fixed.

2 physical states -> 3 effective signed weight levels -> 11.27% accuracy.
16 physical states -> 15 effective signed weight levels -> 96.02% accuracy.

Accuracy difference: 84.75 percentage points.

For W=4, 8 physical states are sufficient to reach the requested 15 signed levels.

Additional physical states beyond that point do not increase precision in this W=4 mapping.

4. BINARY DEVICE ARCHITECTURE TRADEOFF
--------------------------------------

A binary memristor still has only two physical states.
The project does not treat one binary cell as having 15, 63 or 255 physical states.

Higher neural-weight precision is instead created through bit slicing across multiple binary cells.

For W=4: 3 binary slices per differential branch = 6 physical cells per neural-network weight, producing 15 effective signed accelerator levels.

Therefore device technology creates an architecture-level tradeoff: multilevel devices can encode more precision per cell, while binary devices recover precision using additional physical cells.

MENTOR ANSWER: WHAT MAKES THIS NANO?
-----------------------------------

The nanotechnology contribution is not simply the material name supplied to an AI model.

Literature-derived memristor device characteristics constrain the physical representation of neural weights and therefore change accelerator behavior.

The strongest current evidence is physical conductance-state capability: insufficient states severely reduce effective weight precision and application accuracy.

ON/OFF ratio is also an active device parameter, but its importance interacts strongly with ADC resolution.

That interaction makes this a device-circuit-architecture co-design problem rather than simply an AI model ranking material names.

CURRENT LIMITATION
------------------

The present simulator starts from device electrical characteristics rather than directly predicting those characteristics from material physics.

The absolute-conductance experiment also shows that absolute conductance magnitude is currently normalized away.

Thickness, crystallite size, material stack, device area, switching mechanism, line resistance, variability, read noise, drift, filament dynamics, switching kinetics, power and heating are not yet fully propagated into accelerator behavior.

Therefore the current claim should be:

"Literature-grounded memristor device-to-accelerator co-design simulator."

Do not yet claim:

"Full material-physics-to-accelerator simulator."
